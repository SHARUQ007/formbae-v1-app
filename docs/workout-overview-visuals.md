# Workout overview

The overview combines artwork, title, session metrics, target muscles and benefits
in one card, separated by subtle rules. Its short photo uses explicit dimensions
and clipping so bundled images cannot overflow behind the title. The day and
session mode sit on the image. A persistent gap separates the scrolling card
from the compact navigation header. The start/continue action has its own footer, so
it does not cover movement rows or require a gradient.

Six bundled editorial covers rotate across local calendar days. The workout day
ID and session mode choose the starting offset; reopening the same workout on the
same day preserves its artwork. Recovery, mobility, stretching, and yoga sessions
use three calmer covers from that pool. No additional downloads are required.

Three custom SVG sets provide twelve drawings: timers, energy symbols, growth
illustrations, and session paths. The daily selection mixes the sets with the
cover rotation. Warm gold, sage, muted blue, and lilac accents sit on flat dark
surfaces. The anatomical map always shows the actual stored muscle targets;
decorative rotation never changes workout data.

Larger text stacks the metric tiles. The footer grows with its content, and all
exercise rows remain reachable by scrolling. Saved exercise alternatives and
the Continue workout behavior are preserved. Compact movement rows show the
exercise name, sets and reps; rest timing remains in the active session.
